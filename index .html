Viksit Bharat represents the dream of a developed, self-reliant and technologically advanced India. Through innovation, digital transformation and sustainable development, we can create a brighter future for everyone.

Innovations for Tomorrow

☀️ 1. Solar-Powered Car

A solar car is an innovative vehicle that uses energy from sunlight to produce electricity through solar panels.

The electricity is converted into power to run an electric motor, which drives the wheels of the vehicle.

Solar cars can help reduce dependence on petrol and diesel, lowering air pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.

This technology promotes clean transportation and supports India's goal of a sustainable and energy-efficient future.

🚇 2. Modern Metro Rail System

Metro rail is a modern public transportation system designed to carry large numbers of passengers quickly and efficiently within cities.

It operates on dedicated tracks, often using electricity, which helps reduce dependence on private vehicles.

Metro systems reduce traffic congestion, save travel time and help control air pollution in crowded urban areas.

Modern features such as automatic signalling, smart cards and digital ticketing make metro travel safer and more convenient for passengers.

🌱 3. Automatic Irrigation System

An automatic irrigation system uses technology to supply water to crops without requiring constant manual effort.

Soil moisture sensors detect the amount of water present in the soil and send information to a controller.

When the soil becomes dry, the controller automatically switches on a water pump or opens a valve to irrigate plants.

This system saves water, reduces wastage, decreases farmers' workload and improves agricultural productivity.

💳 4. Online Banking

Online banking allows people to access banking services through the internet using computers and smartphones.

Customers can transfer money, check account balances, pay bills and view transaction records without visiting a bank branch.

Digital payment systems such as UPI have made financial transactions faster and more convenient across India.

Online banking supports Digital India and financial inclusion, but users must protect their passwords, PINs and OTPs to stay safe from online fraud.

🇮🇳 The 9 Pillars of Digital India

1. Broadband Highways

Providing high-speed internet connectivity across India.

2. Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity

Bringing mobile network access to rural and remote areas.

3. Public Internet Access Programme

Making digital services accessible through common service centres and post offices.

4. e-Governance

Improving government services through technology and digital processes.

5. e-Kranti

Delivering electronic services in education, health, agriculture and other sectors.

6. Information for All

Making government information easily available to citizens online.

7. Electronics Manufacturing

Encouraging electronics production and manufacturing within India.

8. IT for Jobs

Providing digital skills and employment opportunities to young people.

9. Early Harvest Programmes

Introducing quick digital initiatives to improve public services.
